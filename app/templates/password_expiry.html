Why update passwords regularly?
Updating passwords periodically reduces your exposure if a site you use has been silently breached — many breaches go undetected for months. This is not mandatory, but it is a recommended security hygiene practice, especially for sensitive accounts like banking, email, and work systems.

General guidance (NIST SP 800-63B): You don't need to change passwords on a fixed schedule if they're strong and unique. However, updating after 90 days is a widely adopted baseline for sensitive accounts.
Sensitive accounts: every 90 days Low-risk accounts: 6–12 months Always update if a breach is suspected
